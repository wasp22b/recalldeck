RecallDeck

Flashcards for general science. This app runs a real experiment: half of all students study by recall first, half by re-reading. We measure which group remembers more the next day.

How a session works

  1. One session is 10 cards on general science: physics, chemistry, biology.
  2. From your second session on, the first 5 cards are a retest of cards you saw before.
  3. Your study method is picked for you by a feature flag and stays the same every visit.
